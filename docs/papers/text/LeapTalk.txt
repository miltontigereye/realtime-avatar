                  Smoothness:
Our chunk size is 33 frames. For streaming playback, chunk
i+1 is generated while chunk i is being played; therefore,
smooth playback only requires Tgen /Tchunk < 1. As shown
in Table 8, 33 frames achieves the best Tgen /Tchunk ratio
(0.20), meaning the next chunk is generated within only
20% of the current chunk’s playback time, leaving
sufficient margin for continuous gap-free streaming.
